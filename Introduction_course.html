📚 Introduction

Welcome to the comprehensive online training programme on molecular diagnostics of Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever (CCHF) virus. This course has been specifically designed to provide laboratory professionals in Central Asia with the essential knowledge and skills needed to safely and reliably perform molecular diagnostic procedures for CCHF virus detection.
🦠 About Crimean-Congo Hemorrhagic Fever
CCHF is a viral hemorrhagic fever caused by a tick-borne virus of the Nairoviridae family. It represents a significant public health threat in Central Asia and requires specialised laboratory capabilities for accurate and safe diagnosis. Rapid and reliable molecular detection is crucial for patient management and outbreak response.

🎯 Motivation

This course addresses the critical need for standardised, high-quality molecular diagnostic capabilities for CCHF virus across Central Asian laboratories. By providing comprehensive training on safe laboratory practices, molecular techniques, and quality assurance, we aim to strengthen regional capacity for hemorrhagic fever surveillance and response.
🎓 Learning Objectives
  • ✓ Master safe laboratory practices for handling CCHF virus specimens
  • ✓ Understand molecular diagnostic principles and techniques for CCHF detection
  • ✓ Implement quality assurance and quality control procedures
  • ✓ Interpret molecular diagnostic results accurately and reliably
  • ✓ Apply troubleshooting strategies for common technical challenges
