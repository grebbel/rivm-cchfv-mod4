Several organizations and networks provide **proficiency testing (PT)** and **external quality assessment (EQA)** programs in molecular diagnostics that are active or accessible in the Central Asia region (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and Turkmenistan). These cover both **human** and **animal health** laboratories.

### Major Regional and International Providers

- **Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute (CLSI)** works with ministries of health and laboratory networks in Central Asia to implement international quality standards and improve laboratory performance, including molecular-based diagnostics. Its partnerships provide training and access to PT programs compliant with ISO 15189 and ISO 17043 [1].

- The **FAO Regional Office for Europe and Central Asia**, through the **Central Asia Animal Health Network (CAAHN)**, supports regional diagnostics quality programs for zoonotic and transboundary diseases such as brucellosis. These networks include molecular (PCR) diagnostics and promote participation in PT schemes for regional laboratories [2].

- **WOAH (World Organisation for Animal Health) Regional Representation for Asia and the Pacific** provides a **regional PT network** to evaluate molecular assays (e.g., PCR detection of African and classical swine fever). This program helps harmonize methods and ensures sensitivity and specificity across national laboratories [3].

### Supporting or Linked EQA Networks

- The **EQASIA network** (External Quality Assessment in Asia) is supported by the UK Fleming Fund to strengthen national lab quality systems across South and South-East Asia and is extending collaboration with central Asian laboratories via One Health partnerships. It provides panels for bacteriology and molecular detection relevant to AMR and surveillance [4].

- **PulseNet Asia Pacific**, coordinated by CDC, also extends EQA participation for molecular genotyping and sequencing of foodborne pathogens, which Central Asian public health labs can join as part of international data harmonization [5].

### National-Level Involvement

- **Quality Biology and Production Holding (QBP-Holding)** in Kazakhstan is active in laboratory sciences and operates local quality management and molecular diagnostic research frameworks that contribute to regional proficiency testing collaboration [6].

- **GenoMed.kz**, a national genetics and molecular diagnostics center in Kazakhstan, participates in international quality assurance programs for molecular tests and sequencing-based diagnostics [7].

In summary, the **main molecular diagnostics PT/EQA pathways** available for Central Asia laboratories include collaborations through **CLSI**, **FAO/WOAH networks**, and the **EQASIA** framework, complemented by **local Kazakh providers** and international data-sharing initiatives such as **PulseNet**.
